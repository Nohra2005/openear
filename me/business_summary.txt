OpenEar is a Lebanese online store for open-ear sports headphones. Our mission is to give athletes a seamless music experience: headphones that fit comfortably, never fall out mid-run, survive sweat, rain and water, and keep your ears open to the world so you stay safe outdoors. Every model we sell is tested by our team on real runs, rides and swims before it goes on the store.

We sell four headphone models and one accessory. OpenEar Stride ($79) is our entry model for everyday runners: air conduction, 8-hour battery, IP55 sweat and rain resistance, 28 g, in black or grey. OpenEar Pace Pro ($129) is for serious runners, trail runners and cyclists: bone conduction, 10-hour battery, IP67, 26 g, dual microphones for calls, in black, red or blue. OpenEar Swim ($159) is for swimmers and triathletes: bone conduction, 32 GB of built-in MP3 storage (Bluetooth does not work underwater), IP68 up to 2 m, 8-hour battery. OpenEar Ultra ($189) is for ultra and long-distance athletes: bone conduction, 16-hour battery, IP67, quick charge (10 minutes gives 2 hours), and an ear-hook lock for technical terrain. The Sport Fit Kit ($15) includes spare silicone hooks, swimming earplugs and a carry pouch.

We deliver across Lebanon. Orders reach Beirut and Mount Lebanon within 24 hours and the rest of Lebanon within 2 to 3 days. Delivery costs $4 and is free on orders over $100. Customers can pay cash on delivery or by card. Every headphone comes with a 1-year warranty, and unused items can be returned within 14 days. For anything else, customers can email our support team at support@openear.store or send a DM to @openear.lb on Instagram, and the team replies within one working day.

OpenEar was founded by Rami Haddad and Lea Khoury. Rami is a marathon runner who leads product testing and decides which models make it onto the store. Lea is a triathlete who runs operations, delivery and customer service. What sets OpenEar apart is that we are athletes selling to athletes: fast local delivery instead of weeks of waiting, a short list of models we have personally tested instead of hundreds of untested listings, and honest advice on which model fits your sport.
